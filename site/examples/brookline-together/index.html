✱ The story

A neighborhood shop for a neighborhood that runs on volunteers

Brookline Together is the volunteer-led nonprofit working to keep Brookline vibrant and livable — bringing local businesses and residents together on cleanups, block projects and the Brookline Breeze 5K. It carries on the work of the Brookline Chamber of Commerce and South Pittsburgh Development.

This shop is the wearable side of that. Every print here is a piece of the neighborhood: the cannon, a firehouse, the lettering of the Boulevard.

How a visit goes.

Placeholder stage copy — swap in Lady Moon's real process and policies.

  1. 1.0

    Ask

    Send the idea over: size, placement, references, and the story behind it if there is one. Whoever fits it best writes back.

  2. 2.0

    Draw

    Your artist sketches the piece and sends it across before the day. Changes are part of it — say so while it is still on paper.

  3. 3.0

    Sit

    One chair at a time, no walk-ins in the room. Bring snacks, headphones, and someone to sit with you if that helps.

  4. 4.0

    Heal

    You leave wrapped, with aftercare written down. Questions two weeks later are still free — message the studio any time.
